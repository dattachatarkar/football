Our Story

We're building the infrastructure of tomorrow

RAXOTECH was born from a simple conviction: the best tools should feel effortless. Founded in 2020 by a group of engineers and designers who were tired of clunky, slow, and expensive platforms, we set out to build something truly different.

Today, we serve teams across 180 countries — from scrappy startups to Fortune 500 enterprises — all united by a desire to build, ship, and grow without friction.
